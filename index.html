Why ATS Matters

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software used by employers to filter and rank CVs before a human ever sees them. Over 75% of CVs are rejected by ATS before reaching a recruiter.

Common ATS Failures:

  • • Fancy graphics and tables
  • • Headers/footers with contact info
  • • Non-standard section headings
  • • Missing relevant keywords

Our ATS Solution:

  • • Clean, parseable formatting
  • • Keyword-optimized content
  • • Standard section headings
  • • 95%+ ATS compatibility score

Every NextScale CV is built to pass ATS screening and impress human recruiters.
